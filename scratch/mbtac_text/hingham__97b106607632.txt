MBTA Communities		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         In January 2021, the Massachusetts Legislature adopted an Economic Development Bond Bill that made changes to the state&apos;s Zoning Act. Among other housing-related changes, the law created a new section (Section 3A) of the Zoning Act that requires each municipality in the MBTA district to zone for multifamily housing by right. Failure to comply with Section 3A makes the noncompliant community ineligible for certain state funding programs.  Hingham is presently in  compliance  with the MBTA Communities requirements. Based on the final  Guidelines  issued by the Department of Housing and Community Development, Hingham was required to adopt by-right multi-family zoning in one or more districts consisting of a minimum of 50 acres with a minimum unit capacity of 1,490 units (15%) on the following timetable:&nbsp;
