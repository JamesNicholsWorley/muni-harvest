ARTICLE 11:  ZONING BY-LAW – ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS 
SPONSORED BY:  Select Board  
 
 
 
To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning By-Laws at Sections I-E Definitions, III Use 
Regulations, V-A Accessory Buildings and Structures, and V-G(A) Accessory Family Dwelling 
Unit to reflect amendments to MGL Chapter 40A, Sections 1A and 3 (The 2024 Affordable Homes 
Act) regarding Accessory Dwelling Units (Note: Existing text to be deleted is denoted with 
strikethrough text and proposed text is denoted with bold italic text). 
 
1. 
I-E DEFINITIONS 

===== PAGE 10 =====
 
 
 
10
 
In this By-Law the following terms shall have the following meanings unless a contrary, meaning is 
required by the context or is specifically prescribed. 
 
Accessory Family Dwelling Unit - A dwelling unit contained within or being an extension of a 
single family dwelling to accommodate an additional family only if: (a) a member of the additional 
family is related by the first degree of kinship, marriage or adoption.  
(Added May 1995 – ATM, Article 43) 
 
A self-contained housing unit, inclusive of sleeping, cooking and sanitary facilities on the same 
lot as a principal dwelling, either attached or detached, subject to otherwise applicable 
dimensional and parking requirements, that (i) maintains a separate entrance, either directly 
from the outside or through an entry hall or corridor shared with the principal dwelling sufficient 
to meet requirements of the state building code for safe egress; (ii) is not larger in gross floor 
area than ½ the gross floor area of the principal dwelling or 900 square feet, whichever is 
smaller; and (iii) is subject to the additional restrictions and requirements in Section V-G(A).   
 
Accessory Use - A customary use in conjunction with, incidental to, or subordinate to a use allowed 
by this By-Law; and located on the same lot with the principal use or on an adjoining lot under the 
same ownership. (Amended November 1989 – STM, Art. 7)
