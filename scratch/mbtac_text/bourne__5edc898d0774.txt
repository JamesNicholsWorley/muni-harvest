MBTA Multi-Family Zoning		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         Multi-family Housing Zoning Information (MBTA Ch. 40A Section 3A)  In January 2021, the Massachusetts Legislature adopted an Economic Development Bond Bill that made changes to the state&apos;s Zoning Act. The law added a new section (Section 3A) to the Zoning Act (Chapter 40A of Mass. General Law), which requires MBTA communities to have at least one zoning district of reasonable size in which multi-family housing is permitted as of right.&nbsp;  The MBTA communities law creates zoning that encourages new missing middle housing in areas served by public transit. Housing created under this law provides opportunities for people to live, work and thrive in walkable neighborhoods closer to transit. Bourne is one of 177 communities that are required under the MBTA Zoning Law to create zoning for multi-family housing.  Bourne&apos;s multi-family district must have the following characteristics:   A minimum multi-family capacity of 557 homes  A minimum gross density of 15 homes per acre  The homes must be suitable for families with children  At least half of a compliant zoning district must be contiguous lots of land  All non-contiguous portions of the district must be at least 5 acres in size   The Planning Board has selected the Downtown District of Buzzards Bay for a new Overlay District. In order to be in compliance with the MBTA Communities Act, the Town of Bourne has prepared a Multi-family Overlay District (MFOD) and Zoning Bylaw amendments for review and consideration at the October 20th Special Town Meeting. Below find resources and information on the MBTA communities law. &nbsp;Check back for recent developments. (page last updated on 9/10/25)
