===== PAGE 15 =====
37 Youngs Road (Tri-Town):
Credit for hard work
Established in 2010, the Tri-Town Smart 
Growth District is already medium-density.
Currently zoned at 22 units/acre, it was only 
built out to 14 units/acre.
Using this district lets us get credit for work 
we have already done. Increasing zoning to 
26 units/acre meets our full needs.

9.21 acres
Capacity at 22 units/acre
202 units
Capacity at 26 units/acre
240 units
