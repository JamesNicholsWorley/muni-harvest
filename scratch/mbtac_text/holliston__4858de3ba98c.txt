Article 9: MBTA 3A Zoning – Overlay District 		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         Sponsored By   Select Board   To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning By-Laws by adding Section V-I MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District (MCMOD) to allow multi-family housing as a right as required by Section 3A of the Zoning Act (Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A) and to update the Town&apos;s Zoning Map depicted in Appendix A; or take any other action relative thereto.  Section V-I MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District 
     

 
 
	   

	   

			      
		   				
	 
	
	 	

	 

		 
					 
						 	  Purpose & Intent  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         The purpose of the MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District (MCMOD) is to allow multi-family housing as of right in accordance with Section 3A of the Zoning Act (Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A). This zoning is intended to accomplish the following purposes:   Encourage the production of a variety of housing sizes and typologies to provide equal access to new housing throughout the community for households with a variety of needs and income levels.  Implement the Holliston Housing Production Plan;  Locate housing within walking distance of public transit to promote general public health, reduce the number of vehicle miles travelled, support economic development, and meet community-based environmental goals, including reducing greenhouse gases and improving air quality.  Support public investment in public transit and pedestrian- and bile-friendly infrastructure.  Increase the municipal tax base through private investment in new residential developments.  
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  Establishment & Applicability  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         This MCMOD is an overlay district having a land area of approximately 88 acres in size that is superimposed over the underlying zoning districts and is shown on the map entitled &quot;Town of Holliston- MBTA Subdistricts Location&quot; prepared by JM Goldson (dated August 2024).  An applicant may develop multi-family housing located within a MCMOD in accordance with the provisions of this Section as of right without the need for a Special Permit, Variance, zoning amendment, waiver, or other discretionary zoning approval.  The MCMOD is an overlay district superimposed on underlying zoning districts. The regulations for use, dimension and all other provisions of the Zoning Bylaw governing the respective underlying districts shall remain in full force, except for uses allowed as of right in the MCMOD.  Uses that are not identified in Section V-I are governed by the requirements of the underlying zoning districts.  The MCMOD is composed of two (2) subdistricts as defined by the following Assessor Maps, Block, and Parcels:   Subdistrict  1&nbsp; - Map 14, Block 4, Parcels 4.1, 154.A- Sand 232, Map 14, Block 4, Parcel  1   Subdistrict 2 - Map 5, Block  1,&nbsp; Parcel 21 and Map 5, Block 4, Parcels 22 and 23.)  
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  Definitions  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         For purposes of this Section V-I, the following definitions shall apply. Definitions used but not included herein shall have the meaning given to them under M.G.L. c. 40A or elsewhere in the Town of Holliston Zoning By-Laws.    Affordable Housing Units : A housing unit that is subject to a use restriction recorded in its chain of title limiting the sale price or rent or limiting occupancy to an individual or household of a specified income, or both.   Building Coverage : The maximum area of the lot that can attributed to the footprint of a building (principal and accessory) on that lot. Building coverage does not include surface parking or other impervious area.   Compliance Guidelines : Compliance Guidelines for Multi-Family Zoning Districts Under Section 3A of the Zoning Act as further revised or amended from time to time.   DHCD : The Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development, or any successor agency.   MBTA : Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority.   Multi-Family Housing : A building with three or more residential dwelling units or two or more buildings on the same lot with more than one residential dwelling unit in each building.   Open Space : A designated area on a lot or within a building (or combination thereof) set aside for shared use by residents for gathering, recreation, childcare and other community needs. Open Space shall include required minimum setbacks.  
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  Permitted Uses  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


           Uses Permitted As of Right : Multi-family housing is subject to Site Plan Review per Section VII of the Zoning By-Laws and the associated required parking for that use (surface and structured) per Section V-C Off-Street Parking.   Accessory Uses : Professional office, studio nor home occupation up to 200 square feet with no employees and who do not have regular clients/customers come to the office.  
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  Dimensional Standards  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this zoning, the dimensional requirements applicable in the MCMOD are as follows:     Building   Dimension Size       Maximum Building Height  40 feet    Maximum Stories  3 Stories    Minimum Lot Size  10,000 square feet    Minimum Frontage  50 feet    Maximum Building Coverage  50%    Front Yard Setback  10 feet    Side Yard Setback  10 feet    Rear Yard Setback  10 feet    Maximum Units per acre ( Keynote )  17    Open Space  30%       Keynote : Special Permit required for higher density    Multiple Buildings on Lots : In the MCMOD, lots may have more than one principal building.   Exceptions :  Height. The limitation on height shall not apply to chimneys, ventilators, towers, silos, spires, or other ornamental features of buildings, which features are in no way used for living purposes and do not constitute more than 25% of the ground floor area of the building.  Renewable Energy Installations. The Planning Board as Site Plan Review Authority may waive the height and setbacks defined in this section to accommodate the installation of solar photovoltaic, solar thermal, living and other eco-roofs, energy storage, and air-source heat pump equipment. Such installations shall not create a significant detriment to abutters in terms of noise or shadow and must be appropriately integrated into the architecture of the building and the layout of the site. The installations shall not provide additional habitable space within the development.    
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  Off-Street Parking  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         These parking requirements are applicable to development in the MCMOD.   Number of Parking Spaces : The following maximum numbers of off-street parking spaces shall be permitted by use, either in surface parking or within garages or other structures:     Use  Maximum Space per Dwelling Unit      Studio or 1-Bedroom units  1 per unit    2- or 3+-bedroom units  2 per unit       Number of Bicycle Parking Spaces : No fewer than 5 covered bicycle parking spaces shall be integrated into the structure of the building(s) in projects greater than 10 units. 
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  General Development Standards  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         All developments in the MCMOD are subject to the following development standards regardless of size, building count or subdistrict. These standards shall be considered components of the Site Plan Review process specified in Section VII - Site Plan Review.  Site Design   Connections. Sidewalks shall provide direct connections among building entrances, the public sidewalk (if applicable), bicycle storage, and parking. Connections shall be designed to ensure the safety and convenience of persons traveling within or through the parking area and between the parking areas, the buildings, and the street. Additionally, ADA-compliant sidewalks shall be provided along the entire frontage of the subject property along existing public ways. The Planning Board may approve alternative provisions or waive requirements of this standard in situations where sidewalk construction or use is not feasible or practical.  Vehicular Access. Where feasible, curb cuts shall be minimized, and shared driveways encouraged.  Screening for Parking. Surface parking adjacent to a public sidewalk shall be screened by a landscaped buffer of sufficient width to allow the healthy establishment of trees, shrubs and perennials, but no less than six feet (6&apos;). The buffer may include a fence or wall of no more than three feet in height unless there is a significant grade change between the parking and the sidewalk.  Parking materials. The parking surface may be concrete, asphalt, decomposed granite, bricks, or pavers, including pervious materials but not including grass or soil not contained within a paver or other structure.  Plantings. Plantings shall include species that are native or adapted to the region. Plants on the Massachusetts Prohibited Plant List, as may be amended, shall be prohibited.  Lighting. Light levels shall meet or exceed the minimum design guidelines defined by the Illuminating Engineering Society of North America (IESNA) and shall provide illumination necessary for safety and convenience while preventing glare and overspill onto adjoining properties and reducing the amount of skyglow.  Mechanicals. Mechanical equipment at ground level shall be screened by a combination of fencing and plantings. Rooftop mechanical equipment shall be screened if visible from a public right-of-way.  Dumpsters. Dumpsters shall be screened by a combination of fencing and plantings. Where possible, dumpsters or other trash and recycling collection points shall be located within the building.  Stormwater management. Proposed design shall implement strategies that demonstrate compliance of the construction activities and the proposed project with the most current versions of the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection Stormwater Management Standards, the Massachusetts Stormwater Handbook, Massachusetts Erosion and Sedimentation Guidelines, and, if applicable, additional requirements under the Town of Holliston&apos;s Stormwater Management and Land Disturbance By-Law (Article XL of the Town of Holliston General By-Law) for projects larger than 10,000 square feet.   Buildings: General   Position relative to the principal street. The primary building shall have its principal fa&ccedil;ade and entrance facing the principal street.  Entries. Where feasible, entries shall be clearly defined and linked to a paved pedestrian network that includes the public sidewalk.   Buildings: Multiple Buildings on a Lot&nbsp;   Parking and circulation on the site shall be organized so as to reduce the amount of impervious surface. Where possible, paring and loading areas shall be connected to minimize curb cuts on public rights-of-way.  A paved pedestrian network shall connect parking to the entries to all buildings and the buildings to each other.  The orientation of multiple buildings on a lot should reinforce the relationships among the buildings. All building fa&ccedil;ade(s) shall be treated with the same care and attention in terms of entries, fenestration, and materials.  The building(s) adjacent to the public street shall have a pedestrian entry facing the public street.   Buildings: Infill Lots   If the adjacent buildings are set back at a distance that exceeds the minimum front yard requirements, infill buildings shall meet the requirements of Section 5 Dimensional Standards.  Principal Fa&ccedil;ade &amp; Parking&nbsp;  Parking shall be subordinate in design and location to the principal building fa&ccedil;ade.   Surface parking. Surface parking shall be located at the rear or side of the principal building. Parking shall not be located in the setback between the building and any lot line adjacent to the public right-of-way.  Integrated garages. The principal pedestrian entry into the building shall be more prominent in design and placement than the vehicular entry into the garage.  Parking structures. Buildings dedicated to structures parking on the same lot as one or more multi-family buildings or mixed-use development shall be subordinate in design and placement to the multi-family building(s) on the lot.   Waivers   Upon the request of the applicant and subject to compliance with the Compliance Guidelines, the Planning Board as Site Plan Review Authority may waive the requirements of this section in the interests of design flexibility and overall project quality, and upon a finding of consistency of such variation with the overall purpose and objectives of the MCMOD. 
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  Affordability Requirements  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         Applicability. This requirement is applicable to all multi-family developments with ten (10) or more dwelling units, whether new construction, substantial rehabilitation, expansion, reconstruction, or residential conversion. No project may be divided or phased to avoid the requirements of this section.  In applicable projects, not fewer than 10% of housing units constructed shall be Affordable Housing Units. For purposes of calculating the number of units of Affordable Housing required within a development, a fractional unit shall be rounded down to the next whole number. The Affordable Units shall be available to qualified affordable housing unit purchasers as defined under Section V-Q, that is, households earning income up to 80% of the Area Median Income (AMI) and must be eligible for listing on DHCD&apos;s Subsidized Housing Inventory.  Development Standards&nbsp;  Affordable Units shall be:   Integrated with the rest of the development and shall be compatible in design, appearance, construction and quality of exterior and interior materials with the other units and/or lots.  Dispersed through the development.  Located such that the units have equal access to shared amenities, including light and air, and utilities within the development.  Distributed proportionately among unit sizes.  Distributed proportionately across each phase of a phased development.  Occupancy permits for Affordable Units shall be issued on a pro rata basis along with market-rate units.   Fee in Lieu, Location of Units &amp; Density Bonuses&nbsp;  Affordable Units shall not be bypassed with any fee in lieu contribution, on- or off-site land donation to the Town or density bonus allowed under Section V-Q Inclusionary Housing.    Administration : &nbsp; The Inspector of Buildings shall be responsible for administering and enforcing the requirements of this section.   Affordable Housing Deed Rider : All Affordable Housing Units shall have an Affordable Housing Deed Rider that regulates the future resale of the unit as per Section V-Q (11). This Deed Rider is to ensure that the unit is not converted to a market-rate value unit in the future.
