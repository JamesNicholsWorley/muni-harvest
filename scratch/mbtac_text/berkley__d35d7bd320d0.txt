  Public Forum: Proposed Bylaw Changes (Including MBTA Communities “3A” Compliance)  



  When:  Tonight at  6:05 PM  



   Where:  Berkley Council on Aging, 1 North Main St. 



 The Town will hold a public forum to review and discuss proposed bylaw changes, including the draft  3A (MBTA Communities) compliance bylaw . Community members are encouraged to attend, ask questions, and provide feedback. 



  Materials:  See the attached documents for the draft bylaw language 



  Who should attend:  Residents, property owners, and anyone interested in zoning and land-use updates.
