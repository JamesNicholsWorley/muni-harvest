         In 2020, the Governor signed the MBTA Communities Zoning Law.&rdquo; As Dracut abuts Lowell, which has a commuter rail station, Dracut is classified as an &ldquo;Adjacent Community.&rdquo; &nbsp;  The MBTA Communities Zoning Law requires that each community create a zoning district where multi-family housing is allowed by right; without age-restriction or mandates around unit size or bedroom count. &nbsp; For Dracut the district must create capacity for 1,233 dwelling units.  On June 11, 2026 the Zoning Bylaw Review Committee voted to recommend the Compliant District option shown below to the Board of Selectmen for consideration.
