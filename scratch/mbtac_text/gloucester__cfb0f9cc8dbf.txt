Past MBTA Communities Meetings &amp; Presentation Information		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         The City of Gloucester has contracted with RKG/Innes Associates to assist in the development of zoning district(s) that will comply with the State Chapter 40A 3A Zoning Act and  MBTA Communities guidelines . Please visit this page for updates and opportunities to participate in the zoning development process. At the bottom of this page an action timeline of milestones and future events.   The City Council and Planning Board held joint public hearing on September 11, 2024 and September 26, 2024 and took final action to adopt compliant zoning on October 1, 2024   An attested vote and copy of the adopted Multi-Family Overlay District Zoning Ordinance can be found by visiting:    Approved Gloucester Zoning Ordinance Section 5.30 Multi-Family Overlay District    A recording of the final City Council and where the City Council approved the zoning ordinance can be found by visiting:    October 1, 2024 City Council Meeting Recording    A recording of the second joint public hearing of the City Council and Planning Board can be found by visiting:    September 26, Joint Public Hearing of the City Council and Planning Board meeting Recording    A recording of the initial joint public hearing of the City Council and Planning Board can be found by visiting:    September 11, 2024 Joint Public Hearing of the City Council and Planning Board Meeting Recording   &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;   Meeting Presentation  &nbsp;  The Planning and Development Standing Committee of the City Council, along with the City Council Committee of the Whole met on  September 4, 2024  in the Kyrouz Auditorium to begin its review of the proposed Multi-Family Overlay District zoning ordinance. The meeting presentation and recording of the meeting can be found by visiting:    September 4, 2024 Committee of the Whole Meeting Recording  &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;   Meeting Presentation    A 3rd and final Public Forum to present the draft zoning was held on  August 21, 2024&nbsp; at 6pm in the Rose Baker Senior Center. The meeting presentation and recording of the meeting can be found by visiting:    August 21, 2024 3rd Public Forum Meeting Recording  &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;   Meeting Presentation    At a special meeting of the Planning Board held on  August 8, 2024 , City staff and Emily Innes of Innes Associates made a presentation on draft zoning amendments, which reflect the final zoning scenario developed in previous meetings. The proposed zoning is in the form of an overlay district, which would enable as of right multi-family uses in four defined subdistrict areas. The subdistricts include: the allowance of three family dwellings around the West Gloucester Station and in portions of the R-5 in the Downtown Station Area, and two higher density subdistricts around the Maplewood Avenue area and Schoolhouse Road Area (see draft zoning map). The presentation, draft zoning amendments, draft zoning map and link to the meeting recording can be found by visiting:  &nbsp;  August 8, 2024 Meeting Recording    |    Meeting Presentation    |    Draft Zoning to Comply with Chapter 40A/3A MBTA Communities  &nbsp; &nbsp;  Draft Zoning Map  &nbsp; &nbsp;  At a special meeting of &nbsp;the Planning Board held on  July 25, 2024 , RKG and City staff made a presentation on a refined zoning compliance scenario based on the input from the previous meeting held on July 18th. After discussion the Planning Board voted unanimously to move forward with the draft scenario and directed RKG/Innes Associates to draft zoning text to compliment the scenario to develop a zoning amendment. The presentation (with draft compliance scenario) and link to the meeting can be found by visiting: &nbsp; &nbsp;  &nbsp;July 25, 2024 Meeting Recording  &nbsp;  | &nbsp; &nbsp;  Meeting Presentation with Draft Compliance Scenario  .&nbsp;  At a regular meeting of the Planning Board held on  July 18, 2024 , RKG and City staff made a presentation of a refined compliance scenario based on the feedback to date included most recent input from the Planning Board Subcommittee and members of the City Council Planning and Development Committee members. &nbsp;The presentation and link to the meeting recording can be found by visiting: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;  July 18, 2024 Meeting Recording  &nbsp;  | &nbsp; &nbsp;  Meeting Presentation on Compliance Scenario   &nbsp;  Since Public Forum #2 held on June 12, 2024, a Sub Committee of the Planning Board has held two meetings (on July 2, 2024 and July 9, 2024) to review the feedback on the draft zoning compliance scenarios and to develop strategies for revision. &nbsp;One of the meetings was held as a joint meeting with the members of the Planning and Development Standing Committee of the City Council. Over the next several weeks the City will works with its consultant RKG to develop refined compliance scenarios to be presented at a future meeting of the Planning Board. Recordings of the meeting can be found by visiting: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;   Planning Board Subcommittee Meeting July 2, 2024   &nbsp;|    Joint Meeting Planning Board Subcommittee and the City Council Planning and Development Committee July 9, 2024    On  J  une 12, 2024&nbsp; the 2nd  Public Forum  was held  at Gloucester City Hall  to present  Draft MBTA Communities zoning districts scenarios . &nbsp;At the meeting zoning compliance scenarios and options we presented. The presentation can be viewed at the following link:   Draft Zoning Compliance Scenarios   . A recording of the meeting can be found by visiting:   Public Forum #2 Draft Zoning Compliance Scenarios   .   On  June 6, 2024, Draft MBTA Communities zoning district scenarios&nbsp; were presented at a joint meeting of the Planning Board and Planning and Development Standing Committee of the City Council at a regular meeting of the Planning Board at 5:00PM in Gloucester City Hall. The presentation can be found by visiting:   Draft MBTA Communities zoning district scenarios presentation  . A recording of the meeting can be found by visiting:   Joint Planning Board and Planning and Development meeting June 6, 2024  .  A  Public Q&amp;A Session  was held on  May 21, 2024, from 6:00PM to 8:00PM at Gloucester City Hall.&nbsp; The meeting included a brief   Presentation on the Law, Guidelines and Existing Conditions   followed by an opportunity for residents to ask questions. The meeting was recorded and can be viewed by following this link:  &nbsp;Q&amp;A Session Recording  . The following is an updated flyer with FAQs and the timeline with upcoming meetings and locations. &nbsp;  MBTA Communities FAQs and&nbsp;  Informational Flyer    The first public workshop to identify potential MBTA Communities Multifamily Housing Districts within Gloucester was held on Monday  March 25, 2024, from 5:30PM to 7:30PM at Gloucester City Hall . &nbsp;Information was provided on the State Zoning Act Chapter 40A Section 3A and Gloucester&apos;s and how Gloucester can comply. Please view the  MBTA Communities FAQs . If you have questions or concerns feel free to send the to:  MBTAFeedback@gloucester-ma.gov &nbsp; Addition meetings will be scheduled in late May and June 2024, please check back for updates.  On  August 10, 2022  the Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD) released  final guidelines  for the implementation of new zoning requirements, as mandated by the State of Massachusetts. &nbsp;Please visit  Commonwealth of Massachusetts page at Mass.gov  to read a letter issued by the Housing and Economic Development Secretary, Mike Kennealy.  For more information on the State&apos;s requirements for multi-family housing you can review the  slide show , or watch a one hour  webinar  presented on  September 8th, 2022  by Representatives of the State.  On  September 13, 2022  Mayor Verga issued a memorandum outlining the City&apos;s next steps as they relate to the state&apos;s multi-family zoning requirement. &nbsp;Please  click here  to view Mayor Verga&apos;s memorandum to City Council regarding the final guidelines issued by the Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development.  Please  click here&nbsp; to view the completed Action Plan for MBTA Communities form that was submitted to the  Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development  on January 24, 2023. Please see graphic below for Action Plan Timeline.     On  February 20, 2024 , the Planning Board and Planning and Development Standing Committee of the City Council held a joint meeting to hear a presentation outlining the law, the guidelines and the process the City will undertake to comply with the law. &nbsp;Use the following links to view the meeting and to download the presentation for reference.   Joint Meeting of the Planning Board and Planning and Development - 40A 3A/MBTA Communities Meeting Recording    Presentation by RKG Associates - Gloucester MBTA Communities - Overview of the Law and Compliance
