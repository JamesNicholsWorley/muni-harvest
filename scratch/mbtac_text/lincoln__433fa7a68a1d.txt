Town Vision Statement
Lincoln is a town that cherishes its 
rural, agricultural character, its 
small-town heritage, its open 
space, and its historical legacy. 
The Town is committed to:
“Fostering economic, racial, 
ethnic, and age diversity 
among its citizenry through 
its educational, housing and 
other public policy.”

Requirements Overview
Key Compliance Requirements for Lincoln
●Minimum Zoned Multifamily Unit Capacity: 635 units
●Minimum Zoning District Area: 42 acres
●Minimum % of Zoning District to be Located in Transit Area: 20%
○Minimum 20% of the multifamily unit capacity and the land area included in the compliant district must 
be within a half-mile of Lincoln Station
●Minimum Zoned Density: 15 units/acre
○The district submitted for compliance must be zoned for a density of at least 15 units/acre. Some 
individual subdistricts may be less dense than 15 units/acre as long as all subdistricts calculated 
together are at least 15 units/acre.
●One contiguous subdistrict area must contain ≥ 50% of total district land area
●If making use of the Mandatory Mixed Use provision, ≥ 75% of multifamily unit capacity minimum 
must come from 3A subdistricts alone. All other requirements must be met by 3A subdistricts alone.
●We are allowed to require a minimum of 10% affordable housing
For more information on Section 3A requirements, including key definitions, see: 
○https://www.mass.gov/doc/compliance-guidelines-for-multi-family-zoning-districts-under-section-3a-of-the-zoning-
act/download
○https://www.mass.gov/info-details/multi-family-zoning-requirement-for-mbta-communities
○https://www.mass.gov/doc/advisory-concerning-enforcement-of-the-mbta-communities-zoning-law/download
This is a theoretical zoned 
capacity - not a requirement 
to build a certain number of

===== PAGE 23 =====
Work to Date
• January 2021:  The Housing Choice Act (HCA) signed into law.
• January 31, 2022:  Select Board, Planning Board, and South Lincoln Planning Advisory Committee (SLPAC) briefed on the HCA 
and its application to MBTA communities.
• Fall 2022:  Select Board and Planning Board appointed the Housing Choice Act Working Group, and it began meeting to 
understand the law. Update at State of the Town.
• December 2022:  Town submits documentation for “interim compliance” which has kept us eligible for the state grants that we’ve 
received since then.
• January 2023:  The HCAWG began sending out updates to the town via Select Board newsletters, LincolnTalk, and with posts on 
the Town web page (https://www.lincolntown.org/1327/Housing-Choice-Act-Working-Group)
• May 2023:  The HCAWG reviewed options in preparation for a June multi-board meeting and sent out a postcard letting residents 
know about the June public forums. 
• June – July 2023:  
ü Multi-board meeting with Planning and Select Board
ü Two public forums (June)
ü Neighborhood meetings at Battle Road Farm and The Commons (July)
• September 2023: 
ü Neighborhood meetings at Codman Road and Lincoln Woods; 
ü Public Forum hosted by the First Parish Church.
ü State of the Town on September 30th.
• October 10, 2023:  Multi-board meeting to review the input from State of the Town, which was overwhelmingly in favor of Option C. 
ü The boards agreed to look at whether there might be a viable “Option D” that takes credit for rezoning the Mall area, reduces 
the subdistrict near the train station, and puts some of the zoning in the northern part of town.
• October 24, 2023:  Multi-board meeting
• November 8, 2023:  Two community forums to review 4 Options:  C, D1, D2, D3
