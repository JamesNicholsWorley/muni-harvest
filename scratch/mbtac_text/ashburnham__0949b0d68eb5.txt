MBTA Communities Act Background
Amendment to the MA Zoning Act:
By-Law provides for at least 1 district of reasonable size in which multi-family 
housing is permitted as a right
This is a zoning mandate;  compliance is met whether housing gets built or not 
Multi-family housing shall have no age restrictions
Envisions market-rate housing – not an affordable housing program
Managing housing growth is beneficial
District(s) of reasonable size:
Minimum gross density of 15 units per acre, subject to any further limitations 
imposed by state wetlands and septic laws

===== PAGE 6 =====
Legal Challenges to MBTA Communities
Milton: Challenged legality and applicability of guidelines. Result: Substance upheld, 
guidelines reissued as regulation, compliance date for December 2024 communities 
extended
Holden: Stated refusal to comply, did not submit action plan. Result: Compliance Date 
is December 31, 2025. Lawsuit expected after that.
Middleborough: Sought opinion whether MBTA Communities was an unfunded 
mandate subject to Unfunded Mandates law. AG and Auditor differ. Still in court.
Requires town to affirm that the law is, in fact, a mandate it is required to comply with
Auditor found that writing and passing the zoning amendment, not the fiscal impacts of the 
potential development, is the mandate.
Planning grants are optional, not guaranteed.
Attorney General has asked the courts to appoint Special Masters to write zoning for 
noncompliant communities

Next Steps
