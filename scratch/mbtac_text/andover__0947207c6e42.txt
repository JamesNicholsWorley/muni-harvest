MBTA COMMUNITIES
ZONING
ANDOVER
The Town of Andover is considered an 
MBTA community because of its two 
commuter rail stations. The creation of a 
new zone would require approval by 
Town Meeting. 
The Department of Community 
Development & Planning has submitted 
preliminary reporting to DHCD to comply 
with regulations.
Now, the Town of Andover is seeking 
input from the community to assess 
options for creating an eligible district. 
JOIN THE DISCUSSION:
COMMUNITY CONVERSATIONS
In 2021, the MBTA Communities zoning 
law was enacted in Massachusetts, 
requiring communities that host MBTA 
service to create at least one district in 
which multi-family housing is permitted 
as of right.
The zones must meet the following 
criteria:
The law is intended to confront the 
state’s lack of multi-family zoning, a 
signiﬁcant barrier to the creation of new 
housing, and stimulate housing growth 
in walkable areas closer to public 
transit hubs.
Communities that do not comply with 
these regulations will lose eligibility for 
infrastructure and housing funding
opportunities, including MassWorks and 
housing choice grants.
Minimum gross density of 15 units per 
acre
Located within half a mile of MBTA  
station
No age restriction and suitable for 
families with children.

•	 Multifaceted communications strategy 
began in February in anticipation of 
March 1 Community Conversation.
•	 Annual Town Meeting, scheduled for 
April 29, 2024, has been the anticipated 
end date of communicatiosn strategy.
•	 Given the 14-month duration of this 
outreach effort, the Town has prioritized 
communicating information at strategic 
intervals.
This approach is intended to prevent 
oversaturation of information and fatigue 
among the Town’s audience ahead of key 
moments when public attention is needed.
