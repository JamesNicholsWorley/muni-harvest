What is an MBTA community?
• An MBTA community is a city or town in 
Massachusetts which has MBTA 
services OR abuts another community 
with these services making it an MBTA 
adjacent community 
• Any MBTA community is required to 
have at least one zoning district of 
reasonable size in which multi-family 
housing is permitted as of right without 
need for discretionary approval
• Tewksbury is considered an adjacent 
community

===== PAGE 4 =====
Requirements for all adjacent communities
• Minimum gross density of 15 
units per acre
• No age restrictions
• Suitable for families with 
children
• Districts must comprise of at 
least total 50 acres of land, 
with 25 acres contiguous, 
and no portion shall be less 
than 5 acres
• These new requirements are 
NOT a mandate to build
