				  PUBLIC INFORMATIONAL SESSION  
     3A Multifamily Overlay Zoning Districts  
     Tuesday March 3, 2026, at 6pm  
  Marblehead High School Library   
  2 Humphrey Street, Marblehead MA   
     
  Remote Info:    https://us06web.zoom.us/j/89737422287?pwd=clGAlIovrBLcoTdIJRWZg6E9SoE6JX.1   
  Webinar ID: 897 3742 2287  
  Passcode: 625225  
  Join via audio: +1 646 931 3860 US  
     
  Purpose:  
 Join the Community Development &amp; Planning Department, Planning Board, and Select Board members for an informational session on the proposed 3A Multifamily Overlay Zoning District in Marblehead. The session will cover the proposed locations for the two sub-districts: Tedesco and Broughton Road.
