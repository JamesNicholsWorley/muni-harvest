EOHLC Issues Determination on Shrewsbury’s Compliance with MBTA Communities 
Zoning 
 
TOWN OF SHREWSBURY, MA - Today, the Executive Office of Housing and Liveable 
Communities has issued a letter to the Town of Shrewsbury with its determination that 
the Transit Oriented Development (TOD) Overlay District, or MBTA Communities 
Zoning, passed at the November 18, 2024, Special Town Meeting meets the “substantive 
zoning criteria” of the State’s MBTA Communities Zoning Bylaw (Section 3A and 760 
CMR 72.00).  
 
Secretary Edward M. Augustus, Jr. outlined in the determination letter that, “if the 
action of representative town meeting is approved at the special election,” on February 
4, 2025, “EOHLC can determine that Shrewsbury is compliant with Section 3A.”
