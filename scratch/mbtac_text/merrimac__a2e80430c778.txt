	 MBTA Communities By-Law Meeting 
	 
		  March 10, 2025 @ 6:00 pm  -  7:00 pm  			 

	 
	 
		 
		 
			 
				   &laquo;  CREST 3.10.25  
				  Board of Assessors Agenda 3.11.2025  &raquo;   
			 
			 
		 
	 
	 

			 
			 
			
			 
						 
				 The Community Meeting will be held on  Monday, March 10th at 6PM at Town Hall . It will provide an overview to the MBTA Communities Act and give residents the opportunity to discuss the location for the Zoning District, draft By-Law, and for staff to address questions.  This is the second meeting for this project, a follow up of the 12/10 kickoff meeting held with Merrimac Valley Planning Commission.  The development of the Town’s MBTA Communities Zoning District is funded through a grant received by the Town.
