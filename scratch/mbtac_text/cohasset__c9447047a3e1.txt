B. By adding new Section 300-4.7 to read as follows: 
 
§ 300-4.7 
Additional Use Regulations for the 3A-MFO District    
 
A.   The purpose of the 3A-MFO Overlay District is to allow multifamily housing development in 
accordance with G.L. c. 40A, § 3A and the Section 3A Compliance Guidelines of the Executive Office 
of Housing and Livable Communities (HLC), as may be amended from time to time. In addition, 
Section 300-4.7 is intended to encourage:  
 
1. New or redeveloped housing in close proximity to the Cohasset commuter rail station; 
 

===== PAGE 2 =====
2. A mix of housing sizes and types in walkable areas to promote public health, reduce greenhouse 
gases, improve air quality, and concentrate development where adequate facilities and services 
exist to support it;  
 
3. New investment in housing and mixed-use developments in the Overlay District 
 
B.   Unless otherwise regulated within this Section 300-4.7, all use, dimension, and all other provisions of 
the Zoning Bylaw shall remain in full force. Where the 3A-MFO authorizes uses not otherwise 
allowed in the underlying district, the provisions of the 3A-MFO shall control. 
 
C. All projects proposed within the 3A-MFO District shall be subject to Site Plan Review before the 
Planning Board pursuant to Section 12.7 herein, however, such Section 12.7 to be applied and 
construed in a manner consistent with Section 4A of the Compliance Guidelines of the Executive 
Office of Housing and Livable Communities (HLC), as may be amended from time to time. 
 
D. All projects proposed within the 3A-MFO District shall be subject to all other applicable General and 
Zoning Bylaws of the Town, including, but not limited to, Chapter 223 of the General Bylaws 
(Stormwater Management). 
 
E.  Uses. 
 
(1) Multi-Family Use within the 3A-MFO District is allowed as of right.  
(2) Mixed Use is encouraged to be developed as part of a project within the 3A-MFO District 
especially if a project proposed replaces a pre-existing commercial use.  
(a) Commercial uses, if constructed, shall be constructed on the first floor of Buildings. 
(b) The allowed Commercial uses in a Mixed Use project in the 3A-MFO District would be the 
same Retail, Office and Service uses allowed in the underlying 3A-C District, and additionally 
Home Occupation, and Government Service Provider.

This article implements the state-mandated so-called Housing Choice or MBTA Communities 
legislation. It has five necessary components: 1) designating the area to be zoned and the properties 
included – here, an overlay district upon the commercial district of 3A; 2) adding the overlay district 
to the list of overlay district within Article 3 of the Bylaw; 3) adding necessary bylaw text, as a new 
Sect. 4.7 within the Uses article; 4) adding to the Table of Use Regulations – here by adding a 
separate table for this district; 5) adding to the Table of Dimensional Regulations – here by adding a 
separate table for this district; 6) amending the zoning map to reflect the new overlay district.
