Article 10: MBTA 3A Zoning – by-Law Consistency 		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         Sponsored By   Select Board&nbsp;   To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning By-Laws at Sections I-E Definitions and III Use Regulations as well as delete Section V-G Apartment District Requirements to be consistent with amendments to MGL Chapter 40A, Section 3A MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District (MCMOD) as follows (Note: Existing text to be deleted is denoted with strike through text and proposed text is denoted with  bold italic  text): 
     

 
 
	   

	   

			      
		   				
	 
	
	 	

	 

		 
					 
						 	  I-D Prohibited Uses  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         Private sewage disposal systems or treatment plants are prohibited  except for :  Apartments created under the terms of Section V-G hereof where the septic system is established pursuant to the requirements of the Board of Appeals, the Board of Health and other advisors as defined therein, all according to the terms of the Special Permit allowing such apartments.  Multi-family development projects created under the terms of Section V-I  &nbsp;MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District.&nbsp;  
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  I-E Definitions  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


           Apartment : A multi-family dwelling authorized under Section V-G and V-K, herein.   Lot Area : Area within a lot, including land over which easements have been granted, but not including any land within the limits of a street upon which such lot abuts, even if fee to such street is in the owner of the lot, except that if a corner lot has its corner bounded by a curved line connecting other street lines which, if extended, would intersect, the area may be computed as if such boundary lines were so extended. No more than 10% of any wetland area, as defined in sub-section V-G shall be included in lot area calculations for an apartment development site.   Multi-Family Dwelling : A building containing more than two dwelling units which does not qualify as an apartment building under sub-section V-G. (Amended March 1974 - ATM, Art. 10)    Note : A building with three or more residential dwelling units or two or more buildings on the same lot with more than one residential dwelling unit in each building.  
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  II-A Classes of Districts  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         For the purpose of this By-Law, the Town of Holliston is hereby divided into the following districts:   APT&nbsp;  Apartment District   Section III - Use Regulations&nbsp;   Note : Excerpt of Multi-Page Table.     Use  APT       A. RESIDENTIAL USES   N/A    Multi-family dwellings ( Keynote )  SP    Apartments  SP         Keynote : &nbsp; Except for those multi-family dwelling units permitted by right within MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay Districts subject to Section V-I. 
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  IV-A General Requirements Continued  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         In Apartment Developments, more than one apartment building may be built on a single lot in accordance with subsection V-G. 
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  IV-B Schedule of Intensity Regulations  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


         &nbsp;Minimum Lot Dimensions, Minimum Yard, Max. Height, (Continuous) Setbacks of Building or Structure    District:  Apt. Dist.   Area (s.f.):  10 ac.   Frontage (feet):  500 ( Keynote )   Lot Depth (feet):  500 ( Keynote )   Front Setback (feet):  50   Side Setback (feet):  50   Rear Setback (feet):  50   Maximum Stories:  2 1/2   Maximum Height (feet):  35   Maximum % Coverage (Buildings, Structures, Accessory Buildings):  20   Floor Area Ratio (FAR):  N/A   Exceptions  The above dimensions are subordinate to any Buffer Area requirements. In Apartment districts, the yard dimensions shall apply to building setbacks with reference to: (a) the perimeter bounds of an entire apartment development; (b) each street bounding or within an apartment development; and (c) any private way which, in the opinion of the Planning Board, may later become a street, rather than to each building in such an apartment development. 
     

 
 
				 
					 
						 	  IV-C Modification & Exceptions  
 
					 
				 

 
	     


          In all districts (except Village Center Commercial  and&nbsp;  MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District&nbsp; district s , as provided in Section V-K  and V-I respectively ), 10,000 square feet of lot area shall be required for the second dwelling in a two family dwelling, or a multi-family dwelling. This requirement shall be in addition to the area requirements of the district for any other use.  In all districts (except Village Center Commercial  and&nbsp;  MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District&nbsp; district s&nbsp; as provided in Section V-K  and V-I respectively ), 5,000 square feet of lot area shall be required for all dwellings other than the first and second in multi-family dwellings. This requirement shall be in addition to the area requirements of the district for any other use.&nbsp;   Delete Section&nbsp;  V-D MINIMUM RESIDENTIAL FLOOR AREA in its entirety and remove from the Table of Contents&nbsp;    Delete Section&nbsp;  V-G APARTMENT DISTRICT REQUIREMENTS in its entirety and remove from the Table of Contents
