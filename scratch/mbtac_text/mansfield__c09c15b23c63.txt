         For information on the Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) Initiative: &nbsp;    LINK TO NEW PAGE ON THE TOWN PROJECTS WEBPAGE     The purpose of this page is to describe how the Planning Board arrived at its proposed changes to comply with the MBTA Communities Law. It is important to note that Mansfield has been actively working on implementing zoning similar to what the MBTA Communities Law requires. In fact, in 2014 Mansfield voted to create the Mansfield Station Revitalization Overlay District (TOD) to  &ldquo;promote mixed-use, pedestrian-oriented, high-value development within walking distance of Mansfield&rsquo;s commuter rail station, and to establish traffic patterns that are non-intrusive on existing residential neighborhoods&hellip;Allowed uses are intended to provide a mix of housing types and live/work space, as well as retail, commercial, and office spaces.&rdquo;&nbsp; The Planning Board does not see the law as a mandate but that the elements of it coalesce with the work that the Town has been involved with for over a decade.           The Mansfield Planning Board hosted a&nbsp;   public forum on Wednesday, February 28, 2024   &nbsp;(video link below) to present plans for expanding the Mansfield Station Revitalization Overlay District (MSROD) along the west side of Oakland Street to Chocolate Factory to come into compliance with the MBTA Communities Law. The Board is also interested in implementing additional zoning changes to provide a variety of housing options in Mansfield, as documented in the 2023 Housing Production Plan; and a major discussion point at the Annual Town meeting, which led to the overarching support for the current MSROD bylaw. &nbsp;Other townwide housing initiatives could include amending local zoning to incentivize the provision of starter homes, rental units, accessory dwelling units, family-style units, and multi-family development in key areas throughout town.&nbsp;     Visit our Storymap Website to learn more!   &nbsp;   Your knowledgeable feedback   &nbsp;will be very beneficial to the Planning Board&apos;s work in developing future zoning bylaw amendments on this important issue:    MSROD Expansion &amp; MBTA Compliance:  https://arcg.is/04LSrr     While it is commonplace to be wary of change, especially in the form of a mandate, it can truly become an opportunity to enhance a community if undertaken in a contextually sensitive way. This is where forward-thinking zoning and design guidelines can help reinforce a community&rsquo;s vision. While Mansfield&rsquo;s Master Plan 2020 focuses on preserving open space and historic and cultural resources, it also emphasizes the importance of developing a more diverse housing base, enhancing connectivity among the Town&rsquo;s neighborhoods, and further diversifying the tax base. The MBTA Communities initiative can help address a number of these goals by providing more housing choices essential to attracting a younger demographic as well as providing opportunities for empty nesters to downsize while staying in their community. A mixed-use approach will create the synergy necessary to support local businesses and entrepreneurship if done well.&nbsp;    This is not the venue to debate whether the law is a good idea or entertain political opposition to the law. Please contact your state representatives to express your opposition.&nbsp;  
     

 
 
	   

	   

			      
		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         3A /MBTA Communities&nbsp;  Zoning Phase II:&nbsp;  &nbsp;    MBTA Zoning Initiative &nbsp;   &nbsp;     The Planning Board is continuing its work to study expansion areas for the Mansfield Station Revitalization Overlay District (MSROD). Originally adopted in 2014, MSROD is a zoning overlay district to focus on pedestrian-friendly, higher-density housing, and mixed-use development around the Commuter Rail Station which was updated at the 2023 Annual Town Meeting. This is an effort to strengthen the commuter rail area and Downtown as a whole by  providing  &nbsp;more housing options but it also serves to help Mansfield&nbsp;  comply with  &nbsp;the provisions of the MBTA Communities law. The law requires towns that are serviced by the MBTA to develop zoning districts within a &frac12; mile of the transit station and be at least&nbsp;  50 acres  &nbsp;which allows a certain number of housing units to be built by-right. For Mansfield, this number&nbsp;   is 1,392 units to be zoned by-right, with Site Plan Approval by the Planning Board. The original MSROD area partially meets the required threshold but more area is needed to come  into full   compliance  &nbsp;so the   Planning Board is evaluating areas to the north along the tracks towards the Chocolate Factory and along the industrial area to the south  . These areas are contemplated to allow 15 units per acre, lower than the main MSROD area and more consistent with some pre-existing multi-family developments in these areas. More information, including maps and public information sessions, will be forthcoming.   &nbsp;    MBTA Communities Zoning Work Continues (     LINK TO NOVEMBER&nbsp;  2023&nbsp;  NEWSLETTER      )   &nbsp;    The Town of Mansfield is the recipient of a Community Planning Grant in the amount of $90,000 from the Massachusetts Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC), a grant program first established in 2021 for the purpose of providing technical assistance to MBTA communities charged with establishing a new zoning district in which multi-family housing is permitted. All Massachusetts cities and towns designated as &ldquo;MBTA Communities&rdquo; under Section 30A of MGL c.40A must comply with the regulations by December 31, 2024.  Communities that fail to comply with the Act by this date will be ineligible for any further state grants. Mansfield&apos;s &quot;MBTA Zoning Phase II&quot;  planning grant includes the following:  &nbsp;     Analysis of Zoning and Existing Conditions    Draft Zoning    Design Guidelines    Community Input    Compliance Model Testing    Revised Zoning&nbsp;    Town Meeting    EOHLC Submission    Economic Feasibility Analysis (for Affordable Component)     The Board is interested in complementing the higher density zoning around the downtown area with additional zoning changes to provide a variety of housing options throughout Mansfield such as starter homes, accessory dwelling units, family-style units, and smaller multi-family dwellings.  &nbsp;     In our continued efforts to  reach out  &nbsp;to our residents with the&nbsp;  continuum of  &nbsp;project information related to the Transit Oriented District (TOD) &amp; MBTA Zoning project, we present the following information for your review.  &nbsp;    Please note that as of Fall 2023, the projects have been bifurcated. TOD information has been re-located to the  &quot;Town Projects&rdquo;&nbsp; webpage and zoning work&nbsp;  relative  &nbsp;to the 3A / MBTA Zoning Initiative will remain part of the Planning Board&rsquo;s webpage.&nbsp;    &nbsp;    Current Meetings Summary - up to Annual Town Meeting 5/22/24
