===== PAGE 2 =====
What is the 
MBTA Multi-
Family 
Overlay 
District 
(MOD)?
• Critical housing shortage in Boston area.
• 2021: State law, MBTA Communities Act (3A)
addresses housing shortage. 
• Law requires new zoning districts to allow 
development of new multi-family housing with 
access to public transit. 
Note: this is zoning, not a new project
• Requirements depend on town location/size:
Town with commuter rail
Adjacent  large town
Adjacent small town 
• Sherborn is an “adjacent small town”        
(Framingham, Natick, and Ashland have commuter rail 
stations)
• Sherborn compliance deadline: Dec. 2025.
