                            Remote Meeting 
 
Members Present: Otto Lies, Scott Carlson, Robert Ricker, Nancy Kielinen and James Parker 
 
Not Present: Brynn Zawada 
 
Others present: Pam Harding, Director of Planning; Sara Flagg, Recording Secretary; William Murray, Places 
Associates, Pine Tree Estates; Paul Haverty, Counsel for Places Associates, Pine Tree Estates; Patrick Wood, 
Holden DPW;  
 
S Carlson called the meeting to order at 7:06 pm. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING – DEFINITIVE SUBDIVISION - PINE TREE ESTATES -Holden Pine Tree, LLC – PINE TREE 
ROAD/BAILEYROAD - Assessing Map 172 Parcel 14 and Map 173 Parcels 35 and 62 - 98 dwelling units 
 
P Harding explained that continuance from the last meeting was done to allow time for review of the draft 
conditions and edits that were made.  
 
R Ricker asked about the sidewalk situation. William Murray, Places Associates, summarized that they do not 
agree that the Town has the authority to require the sidewalk nor do they agree with the cost estimate by the 
DPW of the fair market cost of the sidewalk. He cited Sunshine Ridge as an example of a recent development 
with no sidewalk required and stated it would be extremely unfair to require it at Pine Tree Estates.  
 
O Lies referenced that the DPW had made a recommendation for the sidewalk cost to be no greater that 
$262,000 and now it appears to have been revised and he does not agree with that estimate. Patrick Wood, 
Holden DPW explained that the original estimate was based on proportional streets but after confirming with 
the costs provided by consultants they found that the amount should be revised to not exceed $200,000. 
  
O Lies commented that he has always maintained that he is not in favor of waiving the sidewalk. Due to the 
size of the project he would want 2 sidewalks with 24’ width road. If the $53k is given he believes it should be 
added to the sidewalk fund and not the fund for the Bailey Rd sidewalk. 
 
S Carlson commented that his stance is that the Board is required to uphold the safety of residents and he 
would not be supportive to approve this without the sidewalks. 
 
P Haverty stated that it would be beneficial to continue this hearing again to allow time to review the 
comments provided on Friday by DPW. 
 
J Parker asked about the document that Places just received. P Harding said this is the proposed conditions of 
approval which was provided in the packet for the Planning Board. 
 
Motion by R Ricker, seconded by O Lies to CONTINUE PUBLIC HEARING – DEFINITIVE SUBDIVISION - PINE TREE 
ESTATES -Holden Pine Tree, LLC – PINE TREE ROAD/BAILEYROAD AT THE APPLICANT’S REQUEST TO APRIL 26, 
2022 MEETING WITH DECISION BY MAY 10, 2022. Approved by a 5-0 vote
