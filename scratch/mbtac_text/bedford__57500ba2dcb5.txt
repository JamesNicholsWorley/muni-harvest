• Requirement per Massachusetts General Law, Chapter 40A, Section 3A
• Applies to communities with MBTA service and ones adjacent to those that have it
• Zoning requirement not a housing production requirement
• MBTA Communities (which include Bedford) must designate an area 
where multi-family housing is allowed as-of-right.  Requirements include:  
• Land area at least 50 acres
• Allow at least 15 units per acre
• No age restrictions and suitable for families with children
• If multiple areas, one area must be >50% of total district area
• Bedford chooses the location of the zone, subject to State approval

===== PAGE 8 =====
The purposes of the Multifamily Housing Overlay District (MFHOD) 
are to:
• Allow creation of housing as-of-right in a variety of sizes and types;
• Reduce dependency on automobiles by providing opportunities for multifamily 
housing near public transportation, bikeway networks, shopping, and local 
services;
• Preserve open space by allowing new multifamily housing within or adjacent 
to existing developed areas and infrastructure;
• Encourage economic investment in the redevelopment of properties; 
• Expand the customer base for local businesses; and
• Ensure compliance with MGL c. 40A § 3A.
