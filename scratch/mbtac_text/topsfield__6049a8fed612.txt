MBTA Multi-Family District		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         In January of 2021, the MA Legislature enacted &quot;Housing Choice&quot; - Section 3A of MGL c. 40A.  This established the requirement that &quot;MBTA Communities&quot; create a zoning district in which multi-family housing is permitted by right. This new law requires that an MBTA community shall have at least one zoning district of reasonable size in which multi-family housing is permitted as of right and meets other criteria outlined in the statute. For details concerning this legislation, please visit the  Multi-Family Zoning for MBTA Communities  page on the Mass.gov website.
