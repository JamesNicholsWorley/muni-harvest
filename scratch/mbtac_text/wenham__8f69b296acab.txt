BACKGROUND
 
• Economic Bond Bill (House 5250) An act Enabling 
Partnerships for Growth passed the Legislature in 
December 2020. 
• Signed into law by Gov. Baker January 2021
• This Bill was enacted to address the housing crisis 
in Massachusetts. “Between 2020 and 2030, the 
state needs at least 200,000 new homes to 
accommodate growth and achieve a healthy 
vacancy rate.”
• Law included many housing provisions
• Section 18 of the law became MBTA Communities 
Section 3A of the Zoning Act
• DHCD , now known as Executive Office of Housing and 
Livable Communities, published guidelines for 
compliance in December 2021, Revised in August 2023
• Requires all 177 MBTA Communities to create a district 
“of reasonable size” to allow for some multi family 
housing by right.

COMPLIANCE
 
• This is a zoning exercise and Wenham is not responsible 
for creating any housing. We do have to create a district 
and zoning in order to be in compliance.
• Wenham’s current zoning is 97% single family residential and 
 
3% business
• Special Permits allow for different types of housing now
• Senior Living Overlay District
• Flexible Development
• Independent Living Overlay District
• Action Plans were due by January 31, 2023. Wenham has submitted an Action 
Plan which is just a form that indicates that we are working towards compliance. 
Wenham’s Action Plan was approved.
• Final compliance deadline for MBTA Commuter Rail Communities is December 
31, 2024
• Designating a 30A District
• Creating regulations that include requirements for development
• MBTA Communities not in compliance will not receive funds from
• MassWorks
• Housing Choice Grants 
• Local Capital Projects Fund
• Wenham does not currently receive assistance from any of these fund 
sources.
