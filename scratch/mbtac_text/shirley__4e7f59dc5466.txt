4.19. Multi-Family Overlay District (MFOD) 
 
4.19.1 Purpose 
The purpose of the MFOD is to allow multi-family housing as of right in accordance with 
Section 3A of the Zoning Act (MGL Chapter 40A). This zoning allows for as of right multi-
family housing to encourage: 
 
a. The production of a variety of housing types that will provide equal access to new 
housing for people with a variety of needs and income levels. 
 
b. Economic development and meet community-based environmental goals, including 
reducing greenhouse gases and improving air quality by incorporating green 
principles and environmentally friendly best practices. 
 
c. Further public investment in public transit and pedestrian- and bike-friendly 
infrastructure. 
 
4.19.2 Establishment and Applicability

that is superimposed over portions of the Industrial, R-3, and Shirley Village Business Zoning 
Districts as shown on the Town’s official Zoning Map. The MFOD is divided into three

An applicant may develop multi-family housing by right within the MFOD in accordance with

4.19.3 Underlying Zoning.  
The MFOD is an overlay zoning district superimposed over the underlying zoning districts, 
namely the Industrial, R-3, and Shirley Village Business Zoning Districts.  The regulations for 
use, dimensions, and all other provisions of the Bylaw governing these underlying zoning 
districts shall remain in effect. Uses that are not identified in this Section are governed by the 
requirements of the underlying zoning districts.  
 
4.19.4 Definitions 
 
For purposes of this Section 4.19, the following definitions shall apply. All other definitions 
can be found in Section 11 of this Bylaw. 
 
Affordable Unit. A multi-family housing unit that is subject to a use restriction recorded in its 
chain of title limiting the sale price or rent or limiting occupancy to an individual or household 
of a specified income, or both. 
 
Affordable Housing. Housing that contains Affordable Units as defined by Section 4.19.4.  
See Section 11 of this Bylaw for the complete definition of affordable housing. 
 
Applicant. A person, business, or organization that applies for a Building Permit, Site Plan 
Review, or Special Permit.  
 
Area Median Income (AMI). The median family income for the metropolitan statistical 
region that includes the Town of Shirley, as defined by the U.S. Department of Housing and 
Urban Development (HUD).  
 
As of right. Development that may proceed under the Zoning in place at time of application 
without the need for a special permit, variance, zoning amendment, waiver, or other 
discretionary zoning approval with a building permit. Projects built under this Section shall be 
subject to Site Plan Review according to Section 7.0 of this Bylaw. 
 
Buffer zone. A physical divider between two areas that is not developed and serves to 
minimize any unsolicited impacts using vegetation and space.  
 
Compliance Guidelines. Compliance Guidelines for Multi-Family Zoning Districts Under 
Section 3A of the Zoning Act as further revised or amended from time to time.  
 
Dark Sky Compliant.  Outdoor lighting guidelines that aim to reduce light pollution and its 
negative effects on humans, wildlife, and natural ecosystems. 
 
EOHLC. The Massachusetts Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (formerly 
the Department of Housing and Community Development ([DHCD]), or any successor agency.  
 
Multi-family housing. A building with three or more residential dwelling units or two or more 
buildings on the same lot with more than one residential dwelling unit in each building.

Open Space. Undeveloped land, a naturally landscaped area, or a formal or man-made 
landscaped area that provides a connective link or a buffer between other resources. 
 
Parking, structured. A structure in which vehicle parking is accommodated on multiple 
stories; a vehicle parking area that is underneath all or part of any story of a structure; or a 
vehicle parking area that is not underneath a structure, but is entirely covered, and has a parking 
surface at least eight feet below grade. Structured Parking does not include surface parking or 
carports, including solar carports.  
 
Parking, surface. One or more parking spaces without a built structure above the space. A 
solar panel designed to be installed above a surface parking space does not count as a built 
structure for the purposes of this definition.  
 
Residential Dwelling Unit. A single unit providing complete, independent living facilities for 
one or more persons, including permanent provisions for living, sleeping, eating, cooking. and 
sanitation.  
 
Section 3A. Section 3A of M.G.L. Chapter 40A, the MA Zoning Act. 
 
Short-term rental.  An occupied property where at least one room is rented out through 
reservations. Short-term rentals are rented spaces that do not exceed 31 consecutive calendar 
days.  
 
Site Plan Review Authority. The Planning Board is the Site Plan Review authority for this 
Section 4.19. 
 
4.19.5 Permitted Uses 
 
a. Multi-family housing is allowed by right in all Subdistricts of the MFOD following 
the Site Plan Review Process in Section 7.0 of this Bylaw. 
 
b. Where the MFOD is drawn over an Industrial (I) Zoning District, the uses listed in 
Section 3.5.1.a (Shirley Village Business District Permitted Commercial Uses) of 
this Bylaw shall be allowed by right in a building which also contains by-right 
multifamily dwelling units.   
4.19.6 Prohibited Uses 
 
a. Short-term rentals are prohibited in housing built under this Section.

4.19.8 Development Standards 
 
a. Renewable Energy Installations. The Site Plan Review Authority may waive the 
height and setbacks in this Section 4.19 to accommodate the installation of solar 
photovoltaic, solar thermal, living, and other eco-roofs, energy storage, and air-source 
heat pump equipment. Such installations shall not create a substantial detriment to 
abutters in terms of noise or shadow and must be appropriately integrated into the 
architecture of the building and the layout of the site. The installations shall not provide 
additional habitable space within the development. 
 
b. Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations. For all uses within the MFOD, electric 
charging stations are required with one EV space required for every twenty (20) parking 
spaces, rounded up to the next highest number of EV stations. 
c. Buffer areas. Buffer areas to abutting properties shall be retained in their natural 
vegetative state to the maximum extent feasible.  All buffer areas and open spaces shall 
have appropriate native vegetative screening and landscaping to meet development 
standards and design requirements, as applicable. 
d. Outdoor Lighting.  Outdoor lighting shall comply with Sections 4.17.11a.1-4 of this 
Bylaw. 
e. Signs.  All signs shall comply with Section 6 of this Bylaw. 
 
4.19.9 Parking 
The following parking standards shall apply to the MFOD:  
 
a. One- and one-half parking spaces shall be provided for each dwelling unit. 
b. Off-street parking shall comply with Section 5.3 of this Bylaw, Design Standards for 
Off-Street Parking.
