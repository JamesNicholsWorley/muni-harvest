What has Westford done so far?
•	
The topic was introduced to the public at the 
2022 Strategic Planning Retreat 
•	
A public survey was conducted between 
September and October 2022
•	
Based on the initial feedback received, a 
draft Action Plan was submitted to the state 
December 2022, and approved January 2023
•	
A Focus Group of persons aged 55 and older 
was held in May 2023 to hear about some of 
their housing wants and needs 
•	
A Housing Choice Grant was secured in the 
amount of $75,000 to receive assistance from 
a planning consultant, Dodson & Flinker, who 
started assisting the Town in May 2023
•	
A District Local Technical Assistance (DLTA) 
grant was received in July 2023 from NMCOG, 
our regional planning agency, to assist with 
unit capacity calculations using the state’s 
compliance model
•	
The MBTA Communities Multi-family Housing 
Advisory Committee (MCMAC) was created 
in May 2023, with members appointed by the 
Town Manager, with representatives from 
various Town boards and committees, members 
of the general public, and ex-officio Land Use 
Department staff members
•	
	MCMAC held 9 meetings, 1 workshop, and 
2 Joint Discussions with the Planning Board 
from July to December 2023
To learn more about the MBTA 
Communities Law, please scan the 
QR Code or visit this webpage: 
https://www.westfordma.gov/1490/
MBTA-Communities-Act-
Requirement-for-Mul
•	
At the 2023 Strategic Planning Retreat, MCMAC 
and consultants engaged and educated the 
public, and conducted another survey to better 
understand stakeholder priorities and refine the 
proposed district area
•	
An informational session was held in September 
at the 2023 Cameron Senior Center End of 
Summer Party at Kimball Farm
•	
A Public Workshop was held in October 2023 to 
gather additional public input and for attendees 
to explore and model design preferences for 
multi-family housing
•	
A public survey of housing needs for persons 
aged 55 and older was conducted between 
November and December 2023
•	
On December 18, 2023, the Planning Board 
voted to sponsor the proposed zoning bylaw 
amendment
•	
The Planning Board public hearing began 
January 22, 2024
•	
The draft bylaw is being considered at the 
March 23, 2024 Annual Town Meeting
