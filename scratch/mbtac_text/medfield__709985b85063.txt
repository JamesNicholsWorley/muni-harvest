			   UPCOMING PRELIMINARY HEARING: MARCH 28TH AT 7.30 PM, REMOTELY VIA ZOOM (LINK IN AGENDA)     OVERVIEW   The "MBTA Communities Act" is a law that created a requirement that each of the 177 designated MBTA Communities must have zoning that permits multi-family housing by right. (References: MGL c.40A Section 3A; MGL c.161A Section 1)  While Medfield does not have MBTA services within its borders, because it abuts a town that does provide services, it has been deemed to be an “Adjacent Community” that must comply with the law. This means that Medfield must zone &nbsp;50 acres with a minimum unit capacity of 750 multi-family units (this refers to zoning only, which is a legal framework, there is no actual requirement for these units to be  built) .&nbsp;   The Town, through its Select Board and Planning Board, is working with RKG Associates and Innes Associates on analysis associated with compliance. More information on the analysis, findings, and any specific proposals for zoning amendments can be found below.   Zoning changes for compliance will be proposed for May 6, 2024 Town Meeting. Medfield has until December 31, 2024 to coming into complete compliance with the legislation.&nbsp;              UPCOMING MBTA ZONING DEADLINES AND MEETINGS:    - May 6, 2024:  Town Meeting      HELPFUL DOCUMENTS    MBTA Zoning 101 Presentation &nbsp; can be viewed here , a recording of the meeting can be viewed below:   https://medfield-net.zoom.us/rec/share/U-ZKGr-OHZZoTQwkqHMG8zkMXRSoVMks2hGXVTxPN00LAmFMUX5fL6uZlDMOdKD2.owgLX5ONonVprVR7?startTime=1698357477000    Passcode: ^ivR2A7p
