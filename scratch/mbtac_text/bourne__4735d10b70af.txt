SECTION 2110: TYPES OF DISTRICTS 
Amend section 2110 “Types of Districts” by inserting “Multi-family Overlay District 
(MFOD) after the row named Marijuana Overlay District.   
 
SECTION 2140: DISTRICT PURPOSES 
Amend section 2140 “District Purposes" by inserting the following after the Marijuana 
Overlay District description: 
 
Multi-family Overlay District (MFOD): To create a higher density of multi-family 
housing in the Downtown District near public transit to promote an environment 
that is a desirable place to live, work, and socialize. All regulations of the underlying 
district shall apply, except to the extent any such requirement is specifically 
modified or supplemented by the MFOD.
