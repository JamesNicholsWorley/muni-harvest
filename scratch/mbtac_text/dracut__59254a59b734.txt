(i)

===== PAGE 10 =====
Approve a District
Town Meeting can approve a proposal to amend the 
Zoning Bylaw language; the proposed District seeks to 
comply with the requirements of the law in areas where 
actual construction would be very costly and difficult.
Such approval requires recommendation of the 
Planning Board to reconsider within a two year window 
and approval of Town Meeting voters to reconsider 
within one year.
