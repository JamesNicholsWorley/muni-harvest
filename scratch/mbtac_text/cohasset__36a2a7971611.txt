Section 1: Identification
The Section 3A Guidelines establish zoning metrics that apply uniquely to each MBTA community based on its
local transit stations, existing housing stock, population,  and developable land.  This section of the Action Plan
helps to identify the transit stations that determined each community's category.  Appendix 1 of the Section 3A
Guidelines lists each community's category and minimum multi-family unit capacity requirement.
