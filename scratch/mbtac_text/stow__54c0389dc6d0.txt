         &quot;Section 3A&quot; Zoning for Multifamily Housing     Background  The MBTA Communities Act, added to Massachusetts General Laws in 2021, sets requirements for certain towns to adopt a zoning district that allows multi-family housing by-right (meaning, without the need for a special permit, variance, waiver, or other discretionary zoning approval). The zoning must allow for a minimum gross density of 15 units per acre, must not include age restrictions, and must be suitable for families. Stow is considered an &quot;adjacent small town&quot; due to its proximity to a commuter rail station, population, and population density.  Click here  for the basics of this requirement for Stow.  Where are we in the process?  As of May 2026:   Stow is  COMPLIANT&nbsp; with the MBTA Communities Act. To achieve compliance, the Town:&nbsp;  &nbsp;Amended the Zoning Bylaw by adding the Multifamily Housing Overlay District ( Section 5.6 ), and&nbsp;  Added a new Overlay District Map ( Multifamily Housing Overlay District ). &nbsp;&nbsp;    The Zoning Bylaw and the Overlay District Map  meet/exceed the minimum criteria  required by Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A Section 3A (MGL Ch. 40A s. 3A) and Title 760 of the Code of Massachusetts Regulations Chapter 72 (760 CMR 72).&nbsp;  The Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC) has determined that Stow is  COMPLIANT  with MGL Ch. 40A s. 3A and 760 CMR 72. The determination letter  can be found here .&nbsp;  The Attorney General has  APPROVED  Stow&apos;s Zoning Bylaw and Zoning Overlay District as the Bylaw and Zoning District do not conflict with State Law. The approval letter  can be found here .&nbsp;  Prior to approval by EOHLC and the Attorney General, voters at Special Town Meeting (October 27, 2025)  APPROVED&nbsp; the Zoning Bylaw amendments and the new Overlay District Map. To review what was voted on, review the  Town Meeting Warrant .&nbsp;  There are  no additional requirements  for Stow.&nbsp;   For more information, please refer to this  FAQ sheet  and our  Resources  page.
