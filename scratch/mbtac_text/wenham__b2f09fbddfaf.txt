DOES WENHAM HAVE TO BUILD HOUSING IN ORDER TO COMPLY?
     THE TOWN DOES NOT HAVE TO BUILD ANY UNITS.  THE STATE REGULATIONS ONLY REQUIRE
THE   TOWN CREATE A ZONING DISTRICT THAT COULD ACCOMODATE THE UNIT CAPACITY AND
DENSITY REQUIRED
DO THESE UNITS HAVE TO BE AFFORDABLE?
 THERE IS NO REQUIREMENT BY THE STATE TO MAKE THESE UNITS AFFORDABLE BUT, THE TOWN
CAN REQUIRE UP TO 10% AFFORDABLE UNITS
CAN WE ALLOW MIXED USE DEVELOPMENT?
 IT IS NOT REQUIRED BY THE STATE BUT THE TOWN CAN ALLOW IT 
 DO WE HAVE TO ALLOW IT EVERYWHERE WITHIN THE HALF MILE OF 
THE TRAIN STATION?
WENHAM IS REQUIRED TO ZONE A MINIMUM OF 24 ACRES FOR MULTI FAMILY HOUSING, BUT ONLY
20% OF THOSE ACRES HAVE TO BE WITHIN THE HALF MILE OF THE TRAIN STATION. THE REMAINING
80% CAN BE ANYWHERE THAT THE COMMUNITY DECIDES
DO WE HAVE TO CREATE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR THESE NEW DEVELOPMENTS?
THE TOWN IS NOT RESPOSNIBLE FOR CREATING ANY NEW INFRASTRUCTURE, OR FOR PROVIDING
WATER OR SEWER FOR ANY NEW DEVELOPMENT WHERE THE CAPACITY DOES NOT EXIST
 WHAT VOTE IS REQUIRED AT TOWN MEETING IN ORDER 
FOR THE NEW DISTRICT TO PASS?
A SIMPLE MAJORITY VOTE 
WHAT CAN THE TOWN REGULATE?
THE TOWN CAN LIMIT HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS, SETBACKS AND PARKING, AND REQUIRE
AFFORDABLE UNITS AND SITE PLAN REVIEW AND ALLOW FOR MIXED USE 
ALL DEVELOPMENT MUST ADHERE TO CONSERVATION AND HEALTH REGULATIONS 
CAN THE TOWN IMPOSE AGE RESTRICTIONS ON THESE UNITS?
THE TOWN CAN NOT IMPOSE ANY AGE RESTRICTIONS BUT DEVELOPERS CAN CREATE SENIOR
HOUSING IF THEY CHOOSE.
