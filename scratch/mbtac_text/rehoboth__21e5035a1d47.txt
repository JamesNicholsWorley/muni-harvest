ARTICLE 4:  BOARD OF HEALTH-TRANSFER STATION OFFSET RECEIPTS 
SUBMITTED BY: BOARD OF HEALTH 
 
To see if the Town will vote to amend Article 5 from the 2023 Annual Town Meeting to reauthorize 
an “offset receipts” account for the Solid Waste Handling Facility and Recycling Area and its 
operation pursuant to MGL C. 44 S. 53E. Fees collected for the use of the Solid Waste Handling 
Facility and Recycling Area will be deposited in this fund. The account shall be for the expenses and 
improvements incurred for the operation, maintenance, monitoring and / or compliance requirements 
of the Solid Waste Handling Facility and Recycling Area. The Fiscal Year 2024 expenditures shall 
be increased from the estimated amount of $148,700.00 to a new amount of $152,400.00.  All 
expenditures are to be authorized by the Board of Health, or take any other action relative thereto.  
 
___Approved    ___Disapproved             Finance Committee Recommends:  At Town Meeting 
 
Comments: The operation of the Transfer Station is estimated to cost $152,400.00 in FY24 and will 
be funded by pay-as-you-throw fees. This is an amendment from the 2023 Annual Town Meeting in 
May.
