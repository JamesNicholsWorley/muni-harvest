MBTA Communities Overlay District Information&#160;		 
	
		 
			

		   				
	 
	
	 	
 
	     


         As of 05/07/2025 Cohasset has received District Compliance&nbsp;  Project Background   This new law requires that an MBTA community shall have at least one zoning district of reasonable size in which multi-family housing is permitted as of right and meets other criteria set forth in the statute:     Minimum gross density of 15 units per acre    Located not more than 0.5 miles from a commuter rail station, subway station, ferry terminal or bus station, if applicable    No age restrictions and suitable for families with children     In 2020, Governor Baker signed into law a requirement that Cohasset, along with 174 other communities in eastern Massachusetts with access to MBTA service, create a district or districts were multifamily housing is allowed by right. Updated guidelines from the Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (Formerly DHCD, now EOHLC) indicate that Cohasset is an &quot;Commuter Rail&quot; community, and 50% of the new zoning district(s) need to be within proximity of the Commuter Rail Station.  More information from the state regarding this law can be found here .    On March 15, 2023, the   Attorney General issued an advisory concerning enforcement of the law  . By complying with this requirement, the Town will:     remain eligible for important state grants for infrastructure projects;    take part in a regional response to the region&apos;s housing crisis by encouraging development of smaller multifamily housing          Throughout 2023, the Town, supported by resident volunteers in the Zoning Bylaw Committee, has been leading a planning process with to determine the location and details of Cohasset&apos;s district(s).  &nbsp;    The deadline for Cohasset to comply with this law is December 31st, 2024. Our hope is to have the new overlay district accepted at Annual Town Meeting June 3rd, 2024.

Massachusetts Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC) Guidelines : Final guidelines for compliance.   Massachusetts Housing Parntership (MHP) : A round-up of information and resources regarding MBTA Communities.   Massachusetts Housing Partnership (MHP) MBTA Communities Webinar Series : MHP has developed a series of ten webinars regarding the MBTA Communities legislation. All webinars have been recorded, and videos are posted to MHP&apos;s website.
