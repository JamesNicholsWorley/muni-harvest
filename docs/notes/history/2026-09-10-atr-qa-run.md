# 2026-09-10 — pre-2021 ATR QA run

Unattended. No Anthropic API key in the environment, so nothing was parsed or
re-parsed; every record change came from a repair the record settles itself, a
check that was wrong, or a page read here.

## What moved

    published pre-2021 records   808  ->  890
    of the gated set (1,605)     publish 890   review 292   hold 423

89 records added, 6 withdrawn from the published set, 1 withdrawn before it was
added. 60 of the additions came from checks that were wrong; 34 from two new
repairs; 15 from date spellings the bridge did not read.

## Checks found wrong, each against a document first

- **`derive_ballots` picked a count its own record contradicts.** Ashburnham
  2006's page prints "Total Votes Cast = 683"; the derivation read 534 from two
  Planning Board races and reported three correctly-read contests as
  arithmetically impossible. 24 records, 192 contests.
- **A ballot question was asked for a seat count.** 42 records; 27 moved.
- **The bridge did not read three date spellings its own comment describes.**
  "31-Mar-03", "11May2015", "Monday, the Fourth Day of May, 2015". 19 records.

## Checks that were missing

- **Nothing asked what election a section held.** Four state elections and a
  county race were published in annual town-year slots: Ayer 2010 (a September
  party primary), Palmer 2004, Salem 2010 (dated 2018), Plainville 2001.
- **Nothing asked whether the page said any of what the record claims.** Carver
  2014's cut is a financial statement, the report's index and a blank page; its
  nine contests were invented. Of 717 published records with readable sections,
  676 ground at 90% or better and exactly one grounds at nothing.
- **A date six years off its stem was not the fiscal-year offset.** Salisbury
  2010 holds 2016-05-10, grounded 20 of 20.

## Checks examined and left alone

- **"seats inferred as N but the totals imply M"** — 61 records, all in the
  direction the corpus calls legitimate. It is still right to flag: Amherst 2008
  records three seats from three asterisks, and the asterisks are against the
  three LOWEST totals. Not auto-corrected, because 48 contests with a PRINTED
  seat count fall short by the same exact multiple and are correct.
- **A contest whose every row is a role** — 221 in the publish rung, and nearly
  all are genuinely uncontested offices printing only Blanks and Write-ins.
- **Filling a null figure from its own precinct row** — 151 of 206 confirmed by
  the page, but it clears every null in only ONE record. Not worth the risk.
- **Widening the grounding hold above zero** — 30 published records ground under
  60% of their figures and most are innocent: Boxford, Lakeville, Carver and
  Littleton ground every NAME, because their pages are scans with a garbled OCR
  layer.

## The locator

142 reports whose cut held no election were fetched again from web.archive.org
and the State Library and cut with a locator that scores figures-against-names
and the word ANNUAL, both gated on ballot vocabulary being present. 44 now hold
a tally page naming the annual town election, 6 hold a state one, 92 still hold
no tally page. 74 more reports are on municipal hosts and were not fetched.

## Calibration

Re-run over the 1,891 published 2021-2026 records the gate passes 81%, against
the 80% documented before this run. Zero of them name a state or county office.
82 carry contests that disagree about the ballot count; 76 of those print no
Blanks line anywhere, which is the Cummington 2018 shape and benign.
